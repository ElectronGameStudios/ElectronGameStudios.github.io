Veilborn

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026.

This policy applies specifically to Veilborn, published by Electron Game Studios. Other studio games may use different services described in the studio privacy policy.

Your game stays on your device

Veilborn does not require an account. Your companions, progress, settings and purchase-delivery records are stored locally on your device. The current version does not upload your saved game or gameplay analytics to us and does not provide cloud saves.

Veilborn does not include advertising, advertising attribution, session replay, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not request your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. The platform handles payment details; Electron Game Studios does not receive your payment-card information. The app receives product and transaction information to deliver purchased content, restore permanent purchases and prevent the same transaction from being delivered twice on the device.

Apple purchases are checked using StoreKit on your device. Google Play purchases are handled by the native billing service. The current version does not send purchase receipts to an Electron Game Studios verification server.

The stores process information under their own policies: Apple privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

When you contact support

If you email electron.cyber.secure@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as device details, screenshots or an order reference. We use that information to respond and investigate the problem. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information or full purchase receipts.

We retain support correspondence while needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support information. Email is handled by our email provider, Google, under its privacy policy.

Your choices

You can play without making purchases or contacting support. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the app’s local progress, subject to any backups managed by your operating system. We cannot recover a local saved game from our servers. Apple and Google may retain platform transaction records independently.

Children

We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through Veilborn. Purchases are controlled by the platform’s purchase approval and parental controls. If a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and questions

We will update this page if Veilborn’s data handling changes. Questions about this policy or your support information can be sent to electron.cyber.secure@gmail.com.
