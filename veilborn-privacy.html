Veilborn

Privacy policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026.

This policy applies specifically to Veilborn, published by Electron Game Studios. Other studio games may use different services described in the studio privacy policy.

Your game stays on your device

Veilborn does not require an account. Your companions, progress, settings and purchase-delivery records are stored locally on your device. The current version does not upload your saved game and does not provide cloud saves.

Veilborn does not currently include ads, advertising attribution or session replay. Analytics and crash reporting are described below. The app does not request your precise location, contacts, camera or microphone, and does not use advertising identifiers for analytics in this build.

Analytics and diagnostics

Veilborn uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how players move through the game and where they stop. It records app opens and sessions, along with gameplay events such as starter selection, hatching, battles, progression, shop visits and completed purchases. Purchase events include the product and displayed price, not payment-card details or store receipts. Firebase may process an app-instance identifier, app and device information, usage events and approximate location inferred from network information to provide reports.

Veilborn uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes and selected non-fatal errors, including purchase-processing and local-save failures. Reports can contain stack traces, app state, device and operating-system information, app version, a Crashlytics installation identifier and recent analytics events as breadcrumbs. We do not deliberately attach your nickname, email address, full saved game or purchase receipt to these reports.

Google processes this information for Electron Game Studios under its privacy policy and Firebase privacy information. We use it to measure onboarding and retention, improve the game and diagnose reliability problems. We do not use Veilborn analytics for cross-app advertising tracking in this version. Data may be processed outside your country. Google describes Crashlytics crash-data retention in its Firebase privacy information; Analytics retention follows our Google Analytics property settings.

Purchases

Optional purchases are processed by Apple’s App Store or Google Play. The platform handles payment details; Electron Game Studios does not receive your payment-card information. The app receives product and transaction information to deliver purchased content, restore permanent purchases and prevent the same transaction from being delivered twice on the device.

Apple purchases are checked using StoreKit on your device. Google Play purchases are handled by the native billing service. The current version does not send purchase receipts to an Electron Game Studios verification server.

The stores process information under their own policies: Apple privacy policy and Google privacy policy.

When you contact support

If you email electron.cyber.secure@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, such as device details, screenshots or an order reference. We use that information to respond and investigate the problem. Please do not send passwords, payment-card information or full purchase receipts.

We retain support correspondence while needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction or deletion of support information. Email is handled by our email provider, Google, under its privacy policy.

Your choices

You can play without making purchases or contacting support. Uninstalling stops future app analytics and crash reports; clearing app data or uninstalling removes local progress, subject to any backups managed by your operating system. We cannot recover a local saved game from our servers. Contact us about privacy questions or data requests. Because Veilborn has no account system, we may not be able to match Firebase records to a support email. Apple and Google may retain platform transaction records independently.

Children

We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information through Veilborn. Purchases are controlled by the platform’s purchase approval and parental controls. If a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and questions

We will update this page if Veilborn’s data handling changes. Questions about this policy or your support information can be sent to electron.cyber.secure@gmail.com.
