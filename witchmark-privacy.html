Witchmark · com.witchmark.witchmark

Privacy policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026.

This policy applies to Witchmark, published by Electron Game Studios (ElectronGameStudios on Google Play). It describes the planned launch configuration: Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics and Google AdMob, without player accounts or cloud saves. Pre-release builds may not yet enable these services. This game-specific policy takes precedence over the studio policy where descriptions differ.

Local game data

Witchmark stores progress, collections, settings and game state on your device. We do not provide a player login or a studio-operated cloud save service. Operating-system backup services may independently back up app data according to your device settings. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise device location.

Analytics and reliability

Google Analytics for Firebase processes app-instance and device identifiers, app and device information, usage and interaction events, and approximate location inferred from network information. We use these reports to understand use of Witchmark and improve gameplay. Analytics can also record store purchase events when purchase functionality is enabled; these do not include payment-card details.

Firebase Crashlytics processes crash reports, stack traces, app version, device and operating-system information, installation identifiers and diagnostic information to help us find and fix errors. When Analytics is enabled, recent interaction events may be included as crash breadcrumbs. Witchmark does not require your name or email address for gameplay.

Advertising

Witchmark's launch configuration includes Google AdMob. Google's advertising SDK collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers where available, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. Depending on your region, choices and device settings, advertising may be personalized or limited. Device advertising-ID controls are available in Android settings. Disabling personalization does not prevent all advertising-related data processing.

Any consent or privacy controls presented by the app or Google explain the choices available for your region. The final launch version must apply required consent choices before requesting ads. This policy does not itself grant advertising consent.

Purchases

The launch version will offer real-money purchases of virtual currency (Moonstones), which can be spent on randomized virtual cards through Readings. Google Play processes payments under its own policy. The app receives product and transaction information to deliver purchased currency, and Firebase Analytics can record purchase events for analytics. Electron Game Studios does not receive your payment-card details. These purchases do not provide cash prizes or cash-out features. Purchase functionality is not yet enabled in the current prototype. We will update this policy before introducing a separate purchase-verification service or other additional data processing.

Providers, security and retention

Google processes the information described above under its privacy policy, Firebase privacy information and AdMob information. Data may be processed outside your country. Firebase and Google's Mobile Ads SDK encrypt their data transfers using HTTPS or TLS. We do not sell your saved game or support correspondence.

Local progress remains on your device until it is removed or overwritten. Analytics retention follows the Google Analytics property's retention settings; Crashlytics and AdMob retention follows the relevant service's documented practices and settings. We retain support correspondence for as long as needed to answer your request and meet applicable legal obligations.

Privacy requests and deletion

You can contact electron.cyber.secure@gmail.com with the subject “Witchmark privacy request” to ask about access, correction or deletion of information we can identify, including support correspondence. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or full purchase receipts. We may need limited additional information to locate a record and verify a request.

Witchmark has no player account to delete. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes local progress, subject to device backups, and uninstalling stops future app data collection. It does not automatically erase records already processed by Google. Because analytics and crash records use installation identifiers rather than a player login, we may be unable to link those records to an email address. Google's independent advertising and platform records are also governed by Google's privacy controls and retention practices. Some records may need to be retained for security, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Adults

Witchmark is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact support so we can investigate and address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy as Witchmark's services and data practices change. Questions may be sent to electron.cyber.secure@gmail.com.
